How I Work

Curiosity first. Evidence before solutions.

My approach is grounded in curiosity, collaboration, and evidence.

Rather than jumping immediately to solutions, I focus on understanding:

  • What problem are we solving?
  • Who is affected?
  • What evidence do we have?
  • What constraints exist?
  • What does success look like?

Only then do we begin designing.

The result is not simply a better interface. It is a clearer product, a stronger service, and better decision-making.
