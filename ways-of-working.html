Ways of Working

Flexible consulting, facilitation, and advisory support designed around the challenge rather than a fixed process.

Every organisation works differently.

Some teams need a live workshop to build energy and alignment. Others need time to reflect, contribute asynchronously, or receive independent advice before committing to a direction.

The format depends on the shape of the challenge, the people involved, and the decisions that need to be made.

Working Formats

Support can be live, asynchronous, hybrid, or advisory.

Live Facilitation

Collaborative sessions delivered in person or remotely.

Asynchronous Workshops

Structured participation using Loom, Miro, FigJam, and Notion.

Hybrid Engagements

Combining live workshops with asynchronous reflection and decision-making.

Advisory Support

Short strategic engagements providing independent advice, reviews, and recommendations without a full project.

Asynchronous Workshops

Not every workshop needs to happen live.

For distributed teams, busy stakeholders, or organisations working across multiple time zones, asynchronous facilitation can provide greater flexibility while still creating meaningful participation and alignment.

Using tools such as Loom, Miro, FigJam, Notion, and collaborative documents, I design structured workshop experiences that participants can complete in their own time.

This approach allows people more space to reflect, contribute thoughtfully, and engage without the scheduling challenges of a traditional workshop.

Examples Include

Useful when teams need input, reflection, and momentum without another live meeting.

  • Discovery exercises
  • Stakeholder input gathering
  • Journey mapping activities
  • Strategy development
  • Opportunity identification
  • Team retrospectives
  • Prioritisation exercises
  • Decision-making processes

Typical Process

A clear structure keeps asynchronous work focused and easy to complete.

  1. Workshop goals and outcomes are defined.
  2. Participants receive recorded guidance and instructions.
  3. Activities are completed asynchronously over an agreed period.
  4. Inputs are reviewed and synthesised.
  5. Findings are shared through a summary report, Loom walkthrough, or facilitated discussion session.

Benefits

Designed for thoughtful contribution without forcing everyone into the same room at the same time.

  • Works across multiple time zones
  • Allows deeper reflection
  • Reduces meeting fatigue
  • Increases participation from quieter voices
  • Creates a documented record of decisions and thinking
  • Fits around busy schedules

Asynchronous workshops can also be combined with live sessions, creating a hybrid approach that balances individual reflection with collaborative discussion.
